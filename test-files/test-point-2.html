The Point

...is a journey, not just a destination.

Have you ever heard the fable of the Land of Point? Everyone and everything there had a point on the top of their heads. Well, almost everyone. There was a boy named Oblio, and he was round-headed. He didn't have a point. For a while, I felt a bit like Oblio, navigating a world where everyone else seemed to have their "point" figured out—a clear-cut career, a linear path, a single, sharp definition of who they were.

This website is my story of embracing the roundness. It’s a glimpse beyond the polished CV or the curated social media grid. It's a space to explore the winding path that led me here and to share the ideas that are still taking shape. It’s my own version of the Pointless Forest, where everything, in its own way, has a point after all.

This Website & Inklet Lab: A Tinder for Ideas

So, what is this place? Think of it as a personal laboratory. It's my public notebook, my portfolio of experiments, and a standing invitation for conversation. It exists because a resume can't capture the 'why' behind the work, and a LinkedIn profile doesn't have room for half-formed thoughts and audacious dreams.

This brings us to Inklet Lab.

Inklet Lab is the most exciting, and least defined, part of this journey. The vision is to build an ecosystem for ideas to meet, a sort of 'Tinder for collaboration.' A place where a stray thought from a writer could spark a project for a designer, or a problem posed by an entrepreneur could be solved by a developer who just happens to stumble upon it.

Right now, it's just a seedling—an idea I'm nurturing through this website. I'm experimenting, iterating, and figuring it out as I go. Until it grows into that larger vision, this site serves its first purpose: a personal space for connection and creation. A home base for a round-headed traveler in a world full of points.
