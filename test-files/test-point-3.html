What's The Point?

This is a living document, a story, and an experiment. It’s an attempt to capture a journey that doesn’t fit neatly into a resumé, and to plant the seeds for a lab where ideas can grow. Welcome to The Point.

A Fable

You know the fable of the Land of Point? Where everyone and everything had to have a point. It was the rule.

Except for one kid, Oblio. He was pointless. Round-headed. For a while, I felt like Oblio, trying to find my place in a world of sharp angles, linear careers, and clean definitions.

The Path

My journey was drawn by hand, not a ruler. It started with code, but the neatest code is useless if it doesn't serve a human need. That question pulled me into the complex machinery of society—the Indian Civil Services1Often called the 'steel frame' of India, it's the permanent bureaucracy responsible for the administration of the country. A deep dive into systems at a national scale..

Studying policy was learning about why things work. But I was still missing the bridge between the power of tech and the complexity of people. That bridge was economics. It gave me the language of incentives, behavior, and choice—the hidden grammar that underpins both code and communities.

The Why

After my master's, I tried to write a LinkedIn bio. The labels felt like this:

"Software Developer who gets monetary policy?"
"Economist who can build an app?"

A CV tells you what. It doesn't tell you why. I built this site to hold the whole story—the rough drafts, the wrong turns, and the half-formed ideas.
