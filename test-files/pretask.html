Founder for a day — Urban Company 24‑hour plan (Pre‑Task)

The Thesis

Make Urban Company the easiest place for everything home, while keeping service partners motivated. Build loyalty with convenience; build reliability with partner success.

Strategy at a Glance

Three Core Decisions

1) Strategic Vision — “Home 360”

One‑stop platform for the home, starting with a targeted subscription bundle (e.g., cleaning + AC service + priority support).

  • Why: All‑in‑one convenience drives higher LTV and creates a daily habit.
  • Risk: Diluting brand focus. Mitigate with a small, data‑driven pilot program.
  • 24h Action: Form taskforce, define 1k‑user pilot, and set success metric (Target: 20% pilot adoption in 30 days).

2) Operations — Partner Success

Dramatically improve partner earnings and support systems. Launch a dedicated Instant Response Team (IRT) in each key city.

  • Why: Partner success is the engine for service quality; speed and reliability drive bookings.
  • Risk: Higher short‑term cost. Mitigate with performance‑based IRT bonuses.
  • 24h Action: Host partner roundtable (focus on pay structure), shortlist IRT candidates, and draft standby rules.

3) Culture — “One Team” Mentality

Genuinely integrate partners into the company mission through shared goals, recognition, and immersion programs.

  • Why: A shared sense of pride leads to faster issue resolution and higher retention for all.
  • Risk: Can be seen as a PR stunt. Mitigate with authentic leadership participation.
  • 24h Action: Invite partners to the all‑hands, CEO to send note on shared mission, announce "Partner Heroes" award.
