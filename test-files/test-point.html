What's The Point?

Everything has a point, or so they say. This little corner of the internet is mine. It's a place to introduce myself, my work, and a peculiar idea called Inklet Lab. You've arrived at the start of the path.

The Point of Me

I build clear, useful systems where design, code, and research meet. I'm curious by default, rigorous by habit, and playful on purpose.

But that's the short version. The longer story is a winding one, starting with a fascination with old maps and leading through trails of economic policy, data visualization, and web development. The guiding principle has always been to find the simple, human truth in complex systems. This website is a reflection of that journey.

The Point of This Place

So, what is Inklet Lab? It's two things at once:

A Personal Compass

It's my personal website—a living document that shows my work and thinking beyond a CV or a LinkedIn profile. It's a glimpse into how I connect the dots.

A Public Laboratory

It's an experiment in building an ecosystem. A nursery for fledgling ideas, a place for projects to meet and cross-pollinate. Think of it as a "tinder for concepts." The idea isn't fully formed, and that's the point. We'll build it as we go.
